I am Chenyu Zhang (Zachary), an MPhil student in Computer Science and Engineering at
**The Chinese University of Hong Kong**, supervised by Prof. James Cheng. Before starting
the MPhil I spent six months in the same group as a Research Assistant. I hold a BEng in
Computer Science with an extended major in Artificial Intelligence from **HKUST**,
graduated with First Class Honours.

My work centres on **large language model agents** — agentic retrieval-augmented
generation, knowledge management systems, and applying LLMs to software engineering
tasks. Along the way I have also worked on 3D avatar generation, and spent summers as a
software engineer at Huawei and Inspur.

You can find the details on my [CV]({{ base_path }}/cv/), or my code on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Zachary260325). I am reachable at
*ZacharyZhang [at] link.cuhk.edu.hk*.
